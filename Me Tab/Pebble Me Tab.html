Pebble · prototype review

The Me tab

The whole tab. Every row opens its page: Profile, Goal Setting, Health Monitor, Health Reminder (with its reminder and alarm screens), Find Device, Take Picture, Firmware Update, Units & Format, Apple Health, FAQs and About — and the device card opens the device page with shut down, restart and reset behind confirm sheets. The header + runs the pairing flow from onboarding; tapping a dormant band connects it and the card changes to it.

Tap around on the phone, or jump with the panel. Switch the Device card slot to a Pairing CTA or to Disconnected and watch every band page turn into its empty state. Flip a toggle on a band page and it goes pending before it settles; flip one with the band disconnected and it refuses, with the reason.
