The Me tab

The tab shell and all six components, every state — plus two inner pages. S01 Profile: everything onboarding collected about you, and the flow for changing it. S10 Firmware Update: the riskiest page in the tab, in all twelve of its states, from up to date through to a band that drops out mid‑flash.

Tap Profile or Firmware Update on the tab to open them. On the firmware page, Update now runs the real sequence — download, install, restart — and the badge dot on the tab clears itself when it lands. Use the panel to switch the device slot, the dormant bands, the confirm sheet, and every profile and firmware state.
