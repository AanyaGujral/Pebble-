Not in the token set

color.neutral.100 on the chip glyph
The icon inside the chip. color.semantic.neutral.icon (neutral.300) is the token that ought to apply. index.html uses neutral.100, and this file matches the screen rather than quietly correcting it — flagging it for you to call.
96px bottom clearance
The padding that lets the last zone row scroll clear of the floating nav. Carried over from .subpage in index.html; 96 is not on the spacing scale (nearest are 64 and 48).
Motion durations
120ms and 140ms, carried over from index.html. tokens.json has no motion scale. The only animation here is the plot dimming on scrub, and it is switched off under prefers-reduced-motion.
