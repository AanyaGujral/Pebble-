How these are worked out

Steps start from your activity level, then move with any change you make.

Distance is your steps times your stride, which we estimate at 41.5% of your height. Edit either one and the other follows.

Calories is the energy you burn moving — not a food budget. We work out what your body uses at rest, scale it by your activity level, and count the difference.
